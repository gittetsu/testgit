In December 1976, we opened our first store in Nishio City, Aichi Prefecture. Since then, we have continued to open stores as the leading company in the field of prescription dispensing drugstores. We would like to ensure that each of our pharmacists can contribute to each customer in front of their eyes. Spurred by this desire, we have been striving to operate “family pharmacies” loved by local citizens and expanding our operating area to suburbs where patients and customers live. Our basic trading area per store is an area with a 2-kilometer radius where 20,000 people in 6,000 households live.
