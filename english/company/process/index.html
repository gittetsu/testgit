In response to worsening social problems, technological innovation, and changes in consumer values, the SUGI Pharmacy Group operates businesses and provides services in collaboration with its partners, leveraging its strengths and the two drivers of merchandising and prescription dispensing. We aim to grow into a company supported by society by creating new customer value and meeting local healthcare needs and ultimately to realize a society in which all people are happy and full of smiles.
