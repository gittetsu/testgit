Web Accessibility Policy

  • TOP
  • Web Accessibility Policy

SUGI Holdings Co., Ltd. and SUGI Pharmacy Group work to comply with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.1) as well as the Japanese Industrial Standard Guidelines For Older Persons And Persons With Disabilities—Information And Communications Equipment, Software And Services, Part 3: Web Content (JIS X 8341-3:2016 ) in order to ensure, maintain, and improve the accessibility of the websites operated by both companies.

Websites Covered by the Policy

  • SUGI Holdings’s site(https://www.sugi-hd.co.jp/english/)
  • SUGI Pharmacy Group’s Customer Site(https://www.sugi-net.jp/)
  • SUGI Pharmacy’s Site(https://www.drug-sugi.co.jp/)

We will work on the above websites first and gradually move on to our other websites.

Exceptions

This Web Accessibility Policy does not apply to some specific circumstances as exceptions. These exceptions are as follows:

  • Content other than HTML, such as PDF files

    At present, all of our PDF files are not included in the scope of this Policy since bringing all of them into conformance is difficult from the standpoint of technology and information volume.

  • Content, links, and external services provided by third parties

    Some web pages use external services and contain code that we cannot directly manage or control. Such pages are excluded from the scope of this Policy.

Examples of external services:

  • Video sharing platforms, such as YouTube
  • Google Maps
  • Embedding of social media posts

Target Level of Conformance and Degree of Response

Target conformance levels

Conformance to Level AA of JIS X 8341-3:2016 and Level AA of WCAG 2.1 (at the end of June 2024)

Deadline to achieve the target

We will announce the target deadline after completion of accessibility verification (testing).

Scheduled Test Date

An accessibility test is scheduled to begin in June 2024.
