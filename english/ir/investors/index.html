Overview of the Fiscal Year Ended February 2023

In this consolidated fiscal year (from March 1, 2022 to February 28, 2023), the Japanese economy continued to be affected by the novel coronavirus infection. At the same time, there were some signs of recovery in socio-economic activities partly due to the easing of COVID restrictions and border measures. However, the outlook for the economy remains uncertain because of various factors, such as soaring energy and raw material prices, as well as rising prices in step with a rapid depreciation of the yen.
In the drugstore industry, there was a growth in demand for some products partly due to the easing of COVID restrictions and a rebound of COVID-19. Still, the business environment surrounding the industry is becoming even more severe, as indicated by a decline in the prescription unit price, which has been affected by the revisions of drug prices and dispensing fees, a significant reduction in dispensing fees in some cases, and intensifying competition to open new stores.
In the field of prescription dispensing, we strived to address these circumstances by aggressively opening new stores to acquire even more prescriptions. In addition, in order to respond to an increase in the number of prescriptions to be filled by us, we renovated existing stores to enlarge their dispensing rooms or waiting rooms. Also, we encouraged many more patients to download our medication record app to enhance their convenience and began to establish a store network to handle electronic prescriptions, whose operation was launched in January 2023. To improve productivity, we ensured total optimization by assigning medical office workers and realizing the optimum staff assignment for each store.

For sustainability management, we addressed various ESG priority issues (issues of materiality). To contribute to realizing a carbon-free society, we installed solar panels on store rooftops to reduce CO2 emissions per store and promoted the introduction of renewable energy. To contribute to achieving a recycling-oriented society, we collaborated with our business partners and ensured that our stores served as collection bases for PET bottles and medicine sheets, thereby facilitating plastic to be recycled. We also began donations to food banks to help solve social problems, such as food loss and poverty. In addition, in light of the internationally growing awareness of human rights issues and our corporate social responsibility, we have formulated the Human Rights Policy and have begun developing a framework to fulfill our responsibilities in the supply chain.
For the number of our stores, we opened 107 stores and closed 25 stores. To make the existing stores even more competitive, we renovated 244 stores. Consequently, the number of our stores at the end of the consolidated fiscal year was 1,565 (an increase of 82 stores from the end of the previous fiscal year).
As a result of the above, we marked the figures below:
Net sales: 667.647 billion yen (625.477 billion yen year on year)
Gross profit: 202.524 billion yen (191.490 billion yen year on year)
Selling, general and administrative expenses: 170.865 billion yen (159.353 billion yen year on year)
Operating income: 31.658 billion yen (32.137 billion yen year on year)
Ordinary income: 32.391 billion yen (33.082 billion yen year on year)
Profit attributable to owners of parent: 19.007 billion yen (19.389 billion yen year on year)
The business segment of the SUGI Holdings Group is a single segment, so descriptions by segment have been omitted.

Outlook for the Fiscal Year Ending February 2024

Regarding the Japanese economy in the next consolidated fiscal year, the impact of the COVID-19 trend on the economy is expected to shrink, as the Japanese government has announced a reclassification of COVID-19 from Class 2 to Class 5 under the Infectious Diseases Control Law. However, the future outlook remains uncertain due to various factors, such as further increases in energy and raw material prices and a deterioration in consumer sentiment owing to rising prices.
In the drugstore industry, the business environment is expected to remain unpredictable not only because of intensifying competition resulting from companies’ aggressive store openings and M&A but also because of a reactionary decline in sales of products related to COVID-19 and a growing downward pressure from annual drug price revisions.
In this environment, we will strive to improve our profitability by continuing various active initiatives. For example, we will ensure that our product lineup and store environment keep up with changes in the lifestyles of consumers in the recovery phase from the pandemic. We will also enhance in-store operating efficiency to provide customers with even better services and assign staff in light of each store’s situation. In the field of prescription dispensing, where we have secured an advantage since our establishment, we will transfer the handling of drugs to medical office workers, train pharmacists, make full use of digital technology, and ensure closer cooperation with medical institutions. By doing so, we will strive to further increase our productivity and reinforce the quality of communication with customers.
We expect that these initiatives will lead us to mark consolidated results for the next consolidated fiscal year as follows: net sales of 724.5 billion yen (up 8.5% year on year); operating income of 33.0 billion yen (up 4.2% year on year); ordinary income of 34.5 billion yen (up 6.5% year on year), and profit attributable to owners of parent of 20.0 billion yen (up 5.2% year on year).

Notes

The business forecast above is based on the information available as of the day when this material was released. It is possible that the actual business results will be different from the forecast figures due to various factors arising in the future.

Shareholder Benefit Program

Securities code: 7649

  The Company implements a shareholder special benefit plan for shareholders who own 100 shares (one unit) or more and are listed or recorded in our shareholder registry as of the last day of February every year, with the aim of responding to their constant support and deepening their understanding of our products.
